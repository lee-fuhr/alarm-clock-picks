A bad alarm clock can jolt you awake, spike your cortisol, and leave you groggy before your feet even hit the floor. That’s why the best alarm clocks today do more than beep: they simulate a natural sunrise, offer calming wind-down sounds, and sync with your body’s internal clock. Whether you’re a heavy sleeper, a light sleeper, or someone who shares a bed, the right alarm can transform your mornings. When choosing, focus on the brightness range (Is it strong enough to wake you on a dark winter morning?), the quality of the sunrise simulation (Does it fade in slowly enough?), and the alarm sounds (nature sounds beat a harsh buzzer). Consider extra features like sunset mode, smart home integration, and portability. Best for specific needs

Best for heavy sleepers

Its 20-step sunrise smoothly brightens over 30 minutes, mimicking a real dawn. Combined with a sunset wind-down mode, it helps regulate your circadian rhythm without needless bells and whistles. Our pick: Philips SmartSleep Wake-Up Light HF3522/60.

Best for budget-conscious shoppers

The JALL Sunrise Alarm Clock proves you don’t need to spend a lot to get a genuine sunrise wake-up. While it lacks the polish of higher-end clocks, it’s perfect for anyone who wants better mornings without breaking the bank. Our pick: JALL Sunrise Alarm Clock.

Our verdict

Ultimately, the best alarm clock is the one that makes you wake up feeling rested, not startled. For most people, the Philips SmartSleep strikes the perfect balance of effective sunrise, useful sunset, and reliable performance at a mid-range price. If you’re willing to invest in a more holistic sleep routine, the Hatch Restore 2 is a worthy upgrade. And if your budget is tight, the JALL delivers the core sunrise benefit without the premium cost. No matter your choice, upgrading to a sunrise alarm is one of the simplest ways to improve your mornings.
